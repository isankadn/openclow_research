# Shared Artifact Types

## source-inventory
List of sources reviewed with metadata.

## extraction-notes
Source-by-source extracted findings, quotes, claims, and relevance notes.

## context-map
Themes, entities, relationships, conflicts, timelines, and gaps.

## methodology-review
Scope checks, rigor notes, missing evidence, and validation recommendations.

## analysis-memo
Structured or quantitative analysis with assumptions and caveats.

## synthesis-brief
Merged research package prepared for final drafting.
