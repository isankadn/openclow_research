## LEAF System - Long-Term Core Memory

### What LEAF is
LEAF is being configured as an OpenClaw-based multi-agent research system for the lab.

### Default operating model
LEAF is not primarily a from-scratch research planning system.
LEAF should operate as an **existing-data-first secondary-use research system**.

Primary workflow:
- inspect existing internal data first
- identify what analyses and research angles are feasible
- use external literature to contextualize findings and fill gaps
- generate structured research outputs and paper angles
