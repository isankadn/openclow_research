## Interpretation
- Moodle category paths are strong enough to recover year/grade/subject for many older courses.
- Newer courses often sit directly under grade categories, so subject must be inferred from course names.
- This supports grade/level and subject stratification, but classification should be stored with source flags.
- For cross-subject papers, treat subject as a covariate/stratum; avoid pooling math, Japanese, and English as equivalent content.
