# LEAF Agents

## LEAF Conductor
Main orchestrator and synthesis agent.

## LEAF Data Scientist
Central for secondary-use research because internal xPAI and relational data are expected to drive the main analyses.

## LEAF Context Mapper
Maps internal data structures, themes, entities, relationships, and candidate paper angles.

## LEAF Methodologist
Checks rigor, validity, confounds, and evidence sufficiency.

## LEAF Content Reader
Retrieves targeted literature and open-access papers to support internal-data-driven research.
